# Industrie Charts Support & Services

Industrie Charts artifacts are MIT-licensed: usage, distribution, and commercial
deployment are free for everyone. See `LICENSE` and `docs/LICENSING.md`.

The plans below cover **support, maintenance commitments, and SLAs**, not usage
rights. All prices are per product line and per year, in CHF, excluding VAT.

Industrie Charts ships with **156 renderers** across standard, financial,
statistical, specialized, medical, and analysis domains.
